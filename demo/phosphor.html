Born in Beijing, raised in the digital age. To me, code is not merely a tool, but a logical language for deconstructing the world. My goal is to discern patterns within the noise and simulate the texture of reality through algorithms and AI.

From competitive programming to open-source communities, from embedded hardware to AI agents — every line of code is a conversation with the technological world. In Zhongguancun's AI ecosystem, I participate in top-tier competitions and industry forums as one of the youngest voices, proving the power of ideas through real work.

Beyond rationality, sensibility is essential for harmony. In Western pop and rock music, I seek a resonance that transcends the mundane. Music is not just background noise — it is the fuel that keeps thoughts flowing.
